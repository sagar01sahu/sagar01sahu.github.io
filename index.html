Building a RAG app with Spring AI.

Upload a document, ask questions, and get answers grounded only in what you uploaded.

gemini-2.5-flashgemini-embedding-001MariaDB 11.7Java 21top-4 chunks
01
The approach

Grounded answers, nothing invented

The model may only answer from retrieved chunks of your own document.

📥

Ingest

PDF, DOCX or TXT uploaded via REST, text read with Tika, cleaned and chunked.

🧮

Embed

Chunks embedded with Gemini and stored as vectors in MariaDB.

🔎

Retrieve

Question embedded; cosine similarity finds the top 4 chunks.

💬

Answer

Spring AI ChatClient generates the reply with source file names.

02
Rough sketch

The pipeline, drawn first

Two flows: ingestion on upload, and question answering on ask.

Upload file→Tika read→Clean + chunk→Gemini embed→MariaDB vectors
Question→Embed→Top 4 search→Prompt + context→Gemini → answer

06
Field notes

What building it taught me

What Spring AI handled, and what I had to steer.

SPRING AI HANDLED

Pipeline plumbing

Document reading, vector-store integration and ChatClient wiring took very little code.

I STEERED

Embedding dimensions

The vector table's dimensions must match the embedding model, or inserts fail.

SPRING AI HANDLED

Retrieval

Cosine similarity search over MariaDB returned the relevant chunks directly.

I STEERED

Grounding

The prompt had to force "answer only from context", or say it doesn't know.

07
My take

Thoughts on this approach

Where it works, and what is still missing.

✓ Advantages

  • Grounded — answers come from your uploaded content.
  • Sources shown — responses include file names.
  • Simple stack — one Spring Boot app plus one MariaDB container.

△ Trade-offs

  • No auth — all documents share one vector store.
  • No duplicate detection — re-uploads store chunks again.
  • Top 4 only — and no chat memory between questions.
